## 1. Creative Direction: The Living Audiovisual Environment

SONORA is designed not as a static arrangement of digital rectangular screens, but as a:
> **Living Audiovisual Environment**

Music in SONORA is not an invisible file playing behind a passive interface; it is an active physical presence that shapes space, radiates light, vibrates geometry, and responds to human touch.

---

## 2. World Model: Music as Matter

The central conceptual metaphor of SONORA is:
> **Music as Matter.**

In this world model:
* **Audio is Substance:** Sound waves possess mass, velocity, friction, and tension.
* **Tracks are Objects:** Songs are not database rows; they are resonant objects with distinct harmonic geometry.
* **Frequency is Energy:** Low bass frequencies generate deep volumetric undulations; high transients generate sharp, luminous sparks.
* **Fields are Relationships:** Genres, moods, and harmonic keys create gravitational force fields that attract or repel surrounding music matter.
* **Atmosphere is Context:** Ambient color, particle density, and visual depth mirror the emotional tone and sonic landscape of the active performance.

---

## 3. Shape Grammar

Every visual form in SONORA maps strictly to a functional and acoustic purpose:

* **Circles & Discs:** Cyclical time, rotation, decks, loops, and continuous audio playback.
* **Lines & Ribbons:** Waveforms, timelines, signal flow, cue trajectories, and frequency spectrums.
* **Clusters:** Organic groupings of related tracks, artists, and harmonic families.
* **Particles:** High-frequency transients, recommended tracks, micro-samples, and granular audio events.
* **Fields & Meshes:** Atmospheric backgrounds, dynamic EQ surfaces, and multidimensional discovery spaces.
* **Nodes:** Interactive anchor points, cue markers, loop boundaries, and connection vertices.

Random, non-functional shapes and decorative geometric filler are strictly prohibited.

---

## 4. Spatial Composition & Depth Hierarchy

Rather than organizing interface elements using heavy cards and nested borders, SONORA establishes visual hierarchy through spatial physics:

1. **Scale:** The active musical object expands to command the center stage, while secondary context recedes.
2. **Luminance & Brightness:** Active tracks emit clean light; inactive elements fade gracefully into the void (`#000000`).
3. **Proximity & Gravitation:** Tracks with harmonic compatibility (adjacent Camelot keys, matching BPMs) cluster closer together.
4. **Depth Layers:** Layered 2.5D coordinate planes provide clear separation between background atmosphere, stage surfaces, and foreground tactile controls.
5. **Atmospheric Attenuation:** Distant context softens with subtle blur and reduced opacity, drawing focus to immediate interaction.

---

## 5. The Living Player Environment

The music player is not a generic bottom banner. It is the **Living Player Environment**:
* When playback begins, the entire viewport responds to the music's dynamic range.
* Bass transients emit subtle radial shockwaves through the surrounding stage.
* The playhead moves not across a static progress line, but along an illuminated acoustic terrain ribbon.
* Pausing the track causes the environment to freeze in crystalline suspension; resuming restores breathing momentum.

---

## 6. Album Art as an Environmental Catalyst

Album artwork is never treated as a passive thumbnail:
* **Atmospheric Projection:** Artwork colors are parsed in real time, projecting a subtle, harmonically tuned ambient haze into the dark stage void.
* **Visual Energy:** Dynamic contrast, brightness, and particle color adapt to mirror the visual identity of the active recording.
* **Tactile Disc Metaphor:** In deck and vinyl modes, album artwork forms the physical face of the rotating turntable assembly.

---

## 7. Operational Visual States

All primary views and components express clear, distinct visual states:

* **Idle:** Serene, deep void with faint atmospheric particle drift.
* **Playing:** Breathing luminous core, active waveform ribbon, and frequency-reactive meters.
* **Paused:** Still geometry, muted glow, preserved playhead marker.
* **Loading / Decoding:** Coalescing particle vortex indicating buffer ingestion.
* **Seeking / Scrubbing:** Elastic waveform stretching with transient preview and tactile audio scrub ticks.
* **Transitioning (Mixing):** Harmonic overlap between Deck A and Deck B with dual-colored spectral blending.
* **Analyzing:** Off-thread waveform generation visual scanning sweep.
* **Recording:** High-visibility crimson recording pulse with precise elapsed timecode.
* **Editing:** High-precision slicing grid with active region brackets and non-destructive cut lines.
* **Error / Warning:** Sharp amber/crimson signal alerts detailing recovery steps.

---

## 8. Harmonizing Casual and Professional Modes

SONORA seamlessly bridges casual listening and professional audio manipulation:
* **Listening Mode:** Minimalist, cinematic, distraction-free. Artwork, titles, and living waveforms take precedence. Technical knobs recede until requested.
* **DJ & Creation Mode:** Seamlessly expands without navigating away. High-density 3-band EQ, dual sweep filters, tempo faders, and beat markers emerge into the foreground with tactile precision.
* Neither mode feels like a different application; they are simply different zoom levels of the same musical workspace.

---

## 9. Signature Identity

SONORA is instantly recognizable through:
1. **The Pure Void Stage:** Boundless `#000000` dark stage eliminating boxy window constraints.
2. **The Waveform Ribbon:** Ultra-smooth, high-DPI acoustic terrain rendered with precision transients.
3. **Harmonic Constellations:** Spatial discovery networks connecting music by sonic compatibility.
4. **Representation Morphing:** The seamless visual transformation of a track from a search result into an active turntable deck or editable waveform.

---

## 10. Prohibitions & Anti-Patterns

SONORA must strictly avoid:
* Becoming a Spotify clone with identical rectangular cards and green accents.
* Looking like a generic SaaS analytics dashboard with charts and tables.
* Overusing heavy glassmorphism and legibility-destroying blur layers.
* Adopting trendy generic purple/blue "AI gradients".
* Becoming a chaotic 3D WebGL demo that slows down the machine and distracts from music.
* Disconnecting listening from creation into separate, siloed user experiences.
