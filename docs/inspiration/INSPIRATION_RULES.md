## 1. The Core Principle

> **Inspiration is allowed. Copying is strictly prohibited.**

SONORA achieves distinction not by mimicking existing digital products, but by synthesizing proven physical and digital interaction principles into a singular, unified audiovisual medium.

---

## 2. Borrow Principles, Not Pixels

When studying external products or references (as cataloged in `REFERENCES.md`):

### What You May Extract:
* **Underlying Principles:** How latency is masked during seeking; how dual-deck tempo nudging feels tactile; how harmonic compatibility is explained clearly to beginners.
* **Information Architecture:** How queue hierarchies prevent accidental clearing; how keyboard shortcuts facilitate flow state.
* **Physical & Spatial Metaphors:** Rotary knob drag resistance, tape scrub inertia, and gravitational attraction between harmonically compatible keys.
* **Acoustic Physics:** Mathematical relationships between frequency bands, decibels, and visual displacement.

### What You Must Never Copy:
* **Visual Styling & Layouts:** Do not replicate the Spotify 3-column desktop layout, the Serato vinyl skeuomorphism, or the Ableton grey box grid.
* **Proprietary UI Elements:** Do not duplicate brand-specific badges, icons, color gradients, or trademarked aesthetic signatures.
* **Arbitrary Component Kits:** Do not import off-the-shelf component library styling that destroys SONORA's dark-void identity.

---

## 4. Anti-Trend Discipline: Resisting Cosmetic Fads

* **No Trend Chasing:** Never introduce a design trend (e.g., neo-brutalism, extreme glassmorphism, claymorphism, gratuitous 3D meshes) simply because it is trending on design showcases.
* **The Sovereign Question:** For every visual decision, ask:
  > *"Does this strengthen SONORA's unique audiovisual identity, acoustic clarity, and usability?"*
* If the answer is merely *"it looks modern"* or *"it looks cool,"* reject it immediately.

---

## 5. Originality Through Synthesis

True novelty in SONORA emerges from the collision of previously separated domains:
* Streaming discovery meets low-latency DJ performance.
* Tactile hardware synthesis meets fluid spatial particle dynamics.
* Cinematic environmental immersion meets millisecond-accurate audio editing.

The product's identity is found in this continuous, unbroken continuum:
> **Discover → Listen → Analyze → Mix → Edit → Create → Share**

---

## 6. Semantic Meaning & Purposeful Geometry

In SONORA, every pixel must justify its existence:
* A line must represent an audio signal or time horizon.
* A circle must represent cyclical playback or rotational control.
* A glow must indicate acoustic energy or active focus.
* A particle must represent a transient frequency or a floating sample.

Decorative filler that carries no functional or acoustic meaning degrades the user’s focus and weakens the platform.

---

## 7. Usability Remains Paramount

Experimental and expressive design must never compromise foundational usability:
* **Clarity:** Controls must be immediately recognizable and their state obvious.
* **Precision:** Sliders, knobs, and cue points must respond with sample-accurate predictability.
* **Accessibility:** Full keyboard operability and contrast standards are non-negotiable.
* **Responsiveness:** Visual expressions must never cause audio dropouts, UI stutters, or latency lag.
