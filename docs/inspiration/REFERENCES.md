## 2. Reference Domains & Principles to Extract

### 2.1 Commercial Music Streaming
* **Exemplars:** Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Effortless catalog discovery, global search ergonomics, and metadata indexing.
  * Frictionless queue management and playlist curation workflows.
  * Information architecture that balances deep catalogs with quick resumption.
* **What SONORA Rejects:** Rigid card-based grid layouts, static playback progress bars, and the complete separation between listening and music creation.

### 2.2 Professional Music Production (DAWs)
* **Exemplars:** Ableton Live, Apple Logic Pro, Bitwig Studio, Image-Line FL Studio
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Non-destructive timeline arrangement, precision zoom, and sample-accurate slicing.
  * High-density parameter layouts with zero wasted screen space.
  * Direct manipulation of audio clips, automation lanes, and effect chains.
* **What SONORA Rejects:** Cluttered, overwhelming initial screens that intimidate casual listeners; grey, clinical enterprise aesthetics.

### 2.3 Professional DJ Software
* **Exemplars:** Serato DJ Pro, Pioneer rekordbox, Native Instruments Traktor, VirtualDJ
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Two-deck transport ergonomics, cue point triggers, and quantization snapping.
  * High-contrast waveform rendering with color-coded frequency bands.
  * Equal-power crossfader curves, isolator 3-band EQs, and filter sweep dynamics.
* **What SONORA Rejects:** Heavy skeumorphic plastic skins, cluttered multi-window desktop complexity, and lack of ambient visual immersion.

### 2.4 Audio Intelligence & Stem Extraction
* **Exemplars:** Moises.ai, RipX DAW, iZotope RX
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Intelligent harmonic key detection (Camelot wheel) and automated beatgrid alignment.
  * Interactive isolation of musical stems (drums, bass, vocals, melody).
  * Accessible visualization of complex frequency and spectral data.

### 2.5 Hardware Musical Instruments & Industrial Design
* **Exemplars:** Teenage Engineering (OP-1, OP-Z, TX-6), Moog Music, Roland, Native Instruments Maschine
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Tactile, weighted physical metaphors (pots, encoders, switches, faders).
  * Playful, iconic geometric clarity where form expresses electronic function.
  * Premium, minimalist industrial finish with uncompromising build quality.

### 2.6 Generative Visuals & Visual Music
* **Exemplars:** Ryoji Ikeda, John Whitney, TouchDesigner installations, demoscene productions
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Pure frequency-to-geometry translation: mathematical audio reactivity.
  * High-DPI particulate fields and laser-sharp vector ribbons.
  * Atmospheric depth and deep void contrast (`#000000`).

### 2.7 Video Game UI & Spatial Navigation
* **Exemplars:** Diegetic game HUDs (Dead Space, Wipeout, Destiny UI), spatial star maps (Elite Dangerous)
* **Key Principles to Study:**
  * Environmental and diegetic UI that exists as part of the world rather than an overlay.
  * Spatial navigation and fluid coordinate zoom (macro constellation to micro node).
  * Immediate, punchy multi-sensory feedback on interactive triggers.
